==================
A quoted scalar exposes its delimiters and text
:cst
==================
"a"
------------------

0:0 - 0:3   stream
0:0 - 0:3     document
0:0 - 0:3       content: double_quoted_scalar
0:0 - 0:1         opening: quote_open `\"`
0:1 - 0:2         scalar_text `a`
0:2 - 0:3         closing: quote_close `\"`

==================
Quoted scalars retain DEL, C1, and noncharacters as normal text
==================
["ab", 'ab', "￾"]
------------------

(stream
  (document
    (flow_sequence
      (flow_sequence_open)
      (double_quoted_scalar
        (quote_open)
        (scalar_text)
        (quote_close))
      (flow_separator)
      (separation)
      (single_quoted_scalar
        (quote_open)
        (scalar_text)
        (quote_close))
      (flow_separator)
      (separation)
      (double_quoted_scalar
        (quote_open)
        (scalar_text)
        (quote_close))
      (flow_sequence_close))))

==================
Scalar continuation preserves physical source parts
==================
"text\
  continued"
------------------

(stream
  (document
    (double_quoted_scalar
      (quote_open)
      (scalar_text)
      (line_continuation
        (escape_indicator)
        (scalar_line_break)
        (scalar_line_prefix))
      (scalar_text)
      (quote_close))))
